Life Orientation

Standards by which to judge the shape and spirit of the day.

Shine bright as the sun sets
“I shine bright as the sun sets.”

Its meaning is:

  • Stop watching life pass.
  • Convert long-developed abilities into real action.
  • Choose work and activities that make you feel alive.
  • Create things true to your nature.
  • Model a courageous and creative life for Alfie.
  • Build something worthwhile even amid age, fatigue and responsibility.
Alive and peaceful

Value feeling alive and peaceful. Bring passion to the things worth doing. Debora’s expression of this value is: “I don’t go outside the door without passion.”

Excellence, community and resilience

Seek excellence in craft, connection in community, pleasure in the moment, and resilience in adversity.

A stable and interesting life
“An ordinary man, with hobbies. That’s power. Because it’s a better life. Stable. Full of interesting things. Just a beautiful, simple life.”

Connect to the God inside others through music, writing, speech and the other tools available to you.

Current Cumulative Vocal Technique

Use this as the current organising reference for support and practice. It is not itself a completion checklist.

Inhale

  • Traps open to Adam’s AppleMaintained during exhale.
  • Concurrent
    1. Shock inhale to ping-pong balls at the obliques.
    2. Stretch the perineum to the floor until the lower back straightens, directed to the ping-pong balls at the obliques.
  • Then — chest: hands either side of the sternum.

Support Techniques

Optional coordinations and interpretive tools to apply where the phrase, range or sound requires them.

Release and emotion

  • Relax: rear tongue.
  • Emotion / compressor:
    1. Bliss / feel the truth of it; keep the emotion moving even on held notes.
    2. Method singing: take on the character and mood; play a part “like a kitty cat.”

Breath

  • Legato: sing the line on one breath rather than treating every note individually; feel the whole smooth line.
  • Breathiness: use a “haa” feeling.

Vowels and onset

  • Move quickly from the consonant to the vowel; train each vowel and train on “ah.”
  • Use almost non-existent diphthongs and modify or darken vowels as needed.
  • Separate plosives during training—for example, K (quick) — ids—then join them.
  • Caution: avoid guttural or weak starts; the voice begins exactly with the breath.

Vibrato

  • Gentle pitch variation, not tremolo.
  • Submersion, as needed: submerge the sternum for vibrato.

Gesture and crescendo

  • Gesture: gesture the idea each phrase or every other phrase, including through the eyes.
  • Crescendo: bring more power to the top notes.

Tension and naturalness

  • No bodily or mental tension, especially around high A and above.
  • Retain the capacity to turn the head loosely and walk comfortably.
  • Keep it natural and use the mirror to check.

Baggage across the range

Allow the sound and effort to change appropriately as the pitch rises.
