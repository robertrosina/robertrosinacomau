About

Inspired by medieval history and fantasy, The Modern Bard is an acoustic artist whose performances bring together folk song, fantasy ballads, narrative music and songs of the sea. Subtly evoking the bards who stood at the heart of entertainment for centuries, Robert brings the historical tradition into contemporary live performance. His unique tavern repertoire is perfect for historic pubs, breweries, taprooms, rustic weddings and themed restaurants.

  • 70+ song repertoire spanning traditional folk, sea songs, fantasy music and ballads
  • Voice, acoustic guitar and ukulele
  • Up to 3 × 50-minute sets, with shorter feature sets and tailored performance lengths available
  • Special song requests available with advance notice
  • Suitable for pubs, breweries, weddings, festivals, private events and themed entertainment
  • Own PA available
  • Public liability insured
  • Based in Sydney and the Hunter Valley, available across NSW
